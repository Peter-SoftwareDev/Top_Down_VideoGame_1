# Characters and Gear

## How it works: paper-doll layers
Every human uses the same rig: 24×32 frames, four directions (left is right mirrored), with **idle, walk, crouch and attack** frames. Each character is a stack of layers that line up frame for frame:

| Layer | Tiers |
|---|---|
| **body** | `prisoner` (rags, bare feet), `guard` (pale, grey) |
| **back** | 1 tattered cloak · 2 Gaolwarden red cloak · 3 Rift-teal mantle with bronze trim |
| **head** | 1 hood · 2 Gaolwarden iron helm · 3 imperial bronze helm with a red crest |
| **torso** | 1 scavenged leather jerkin · 2 mail with a Gaolwarden tabard · 3 bronze cuirass with a Rift-glass inset |
| **hands** | 0 broken shackles · 1 cloth wraps · 2 leather gloves · 3 bronze gauntlets |
| **feet** | 1 rag wraps · 2 leather boots · 3 iron greaves |

At runtime `scripts/CharacterSprite.gd` stacks the equipped layers in the right order for each facing (the cloak goes *behind* you facing down and *over* your back facing up), outlines each frame, builds a normal map for torchlight, and caches the result. **Any combination works.** Find boots, and boots appear.

The four test sets (press **G**): **Prisoner → Scavenger → Turncoat → Relic-bearer.** It's a visual story of the run: from rags and broken shackles, through scavenged leather and stolen guard gear, to relics of the fallen empire.

Guards are the same rig in Gaolwarden gear, which is why the **Turncoat** set looks like a guard. The Gaoler and the hounds have their own sheets.

## Gear that matters in play (ideas)
- **Weight vs. stealth:** iron greaves and a cuirass make footsteps louder; rag wraps and a cloak make them quieter. Armour becomes a trade-off, not a pure upgrade.
- **The Turncoat disguise:** in full Gaolwarden gear, guards take longer to notice you at a distance, but anyone who sees your face up close knows. It's the only "social stealth" this setting really supports.
- **Visible wear:** gear dents, tears and darkens as it takes hits (the design's equipment wear, shown on the sprite).
- **Grime and blood:** your rags get dirtier the deeper you go, and floor and water stains show on your boots.
- **Rift-touched relics:** tier-3 gear faintly glows near the Rift, a hint of its origin and of how dangerous it might be to wear.

## Hand-off to an artist
The layers are generated by `tools/pixelart/characters.py` as placeholders. An artist can redraw any layer PNG in `assets/art/chars/human/` on the same frame grid (4 columns × 12 rows of 24×32) using the palette in `assets/art/style_test/palette.gpl`. The game picks the new art up without code changes. **Draw the prisoner's body first:** it's the template every other layer sits on.
