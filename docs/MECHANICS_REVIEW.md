# Mechanics Review: What to Borrow, Tuned to This Game

What you've shown you care about: **physics-driven combat** (kicks, pits, chains, crates), **stealth** (sight, light, crouch, noise), **high-stakes runs** (death costs a lot, risky optional rooms), and a **gloomy, hand-made world**. Below are common mechanics from the genres closest to that. For each: what it is, how it would fit here, and a verdict.

## 1. Stealth games
| Mechanic | Fit here | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Last known position:** guards search where they *think* you are; a ghost marker shows you that spot. | Makes outsmarting guards readable. It pairs with the suspicion meter and rocks. | **Do it.** Cheap and big. |
| **Body discovery:** guards find bodies and raise the alarm. | *Bodies in the Drop or in water are never found.* This makes physics kills matter for stealth. | **Do it.** |
| **Guard communication:** shouts, bells and horns. | Shouts and barks exist already; the Bell-warden extends this. | Next step. |
| **Footstep noise by surface:** puddles, rubble and grates are loud. | Ties sound to level design. | Later, once there's audio. |
| **Disguises / social stealth.** | Doesn't fit a lone prisoner in a monster-run fortress. | Skip. |

## 2. Immersive sims (systems that combine)
| Mechanic | Fit here | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Systemic elements:** oil spreads, fire burns oil and wood, water puts fire out. | The Foundry and the "fire" ideas are already in the doc. Kicking a burning crate into oil is the chain reaction you want. | **Do it,** as one element first (fire). |
| **Several solutions per room:** every main route can be sneaked, fought or engineered. | Already your design principle. Make it a level-design checklist. | Adopt as a rule. |
| **Environmental storytelling:** prisoner scratchings, guard notes, sentencing carvings. | Huge for the setting (WORLD.md) at little gameplay cost. | Yes, with the art pass. |

## 3. Roguelites (runs, meta-progression)
| Mechanic | Fit here | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Vows / curses:** take a handicap for a better reward ("guards are wary from the start: +50% Tallow"). | It *is* your core question ("risk it for loot?"), applied to whole floors. | **Do it** for floor rewards. |
| **Relics with synergies:** items that change rules ("kicks ignite", "stunned guards spill Tallow"). | Gives runs identity without big skill trees. | Yes, a small set first. |
| **Refuges / shops between floors.** | A quiet room, a fellow prisoner selling things for Tallow. Breathing room in a tense game. | Yes, with M5. |
| **Corpse run:** your remains wait where you died, holding part of your lost Tallow. | High stakes with a sliver of hope. | Maybe. Test it. |
| **Daily seeded runs.** | Community feature, later. | Later. |

## 4. Action games (combat feel)
| Mechanic | Fit here | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Parry:** a slash in the last moment of a wind-up staggers. | Gives the Blade a skill ceiling. Telegraphs already exist. | **Do it.** |
| **Perfect dodge:** dodging *through* an attack at the last moment slows time briefly. | Rewards reading telegraphs. Reuses the existing slow-motion. | Yes. |
| **Charged kick:** hold to push further. | More expressive physics. | Try it. |
| **Stamina for everything.** | You preferred cooldowns, and they're working. | Skip. |

## 5. Ideas specific to this game
| Mechanic | Why it fits | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **A tallow lantern you can light.** Deep floors are pitch dark. Light your lantern to see, and guards can see you too. It burns Tallow. | Turns light and shadow into a resource decision every minute. Strong theme ("your light lets you see; it lets them see you"). | **Top pick.** |
| **Free fellow prisoners.** Optional cells hold prisoners. Freed, they follow you (and can be spotted), and reaching the exit with them earns Embers or unlocks refuge vendors. | Risk-reward escort that echoes the family story. | **Top pick.** |
| **The Drop remembers.** Guards you knock into a pit are found, broken, on the floor below, with your bolts and their loot. | Rewards physics play across floors and sells "the Drop". | Yes, when floors exist. |
| **Drop-rope:** a rare tool to *choose* to take the Drop, skipping a floor's exit at a cost. | A high-risk shortcut, true to the setting. | Later. |
| **A hand-drawn map.** The prisoner sketches the floor as you explore: an art showcase and navigation in one. | Fits the "hand-worked" goal. | With the art pass. |

## Recommended order
1. **Last known position + body discovery** (stealth readability, and a reason for physics kills).
2. **The tallow lantern** (the game's most distinctive mechanic).
3. **Parry + perfect dodge** (combat skill expression for both loadouts).
4. **Fire as the first systemic element** (oil, burning crates, braziers you can tip over).
5. **Freeing prisoners**, then **vows and relics** when the run structure (M5) is built.
