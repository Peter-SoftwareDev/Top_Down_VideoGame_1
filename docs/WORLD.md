# The World

## The journey in one line
A prisoner escapes **Gloamhold**, a fortress built down the wall of **the Rift**, and descends into **the Hollowreach**, a hidden underground world, to reach **the Ashen Delvings**, the old mines that lead home to **Hearthmere**.

## Places (top to bottom)

| Name | What it is | Role in the game |
|---|---|---|
| **The Sunken Lands** | The sealed dead lands around the Rift. | Where the prisoner was lured. Seen only from above. |
| **The surface beyond** | Held by **orc warbands** (working name: *the Ironjaw host*) and flying beasts. | Why going up is death. Felt as a threat, rarely seen. |
| **Wyrmkites** | Lean flying beasts that hunt in flocks and nest in the Rift's upper walls. | A future enemy: they swoop across chasms and open-air floors. |
| **Carrion drakes** | Huge flying beasts that circle the Rift. | Atmosphere and dread: their shadows pass over the upper floors. A possible set piece. |
| **The Rift** *(new: "the Sunder")* | The vast chasm the fortress clings to. | Every pit in the game is a window into it. "Taking the Drop" means falling into it. |
| **Gloamhold** | The fortress built down the Rift's wall by a fallen empire, later a prison. | **Act 1:** the three-floor prototype (Prison, Cistern, Foundry). |
| **The Lowmarch** | The borderland where Gloamhold's foundations break into the caves: broken watch-posts, rope bridges, old border gates. | **Act 2:** half fortress, half cavern. The Rift is wide open here, and wyrmkites roost. |
| **The Hollowreach** | The whole underground world. | The name everyone uses for "down there". |
| **The Undervault** | The Hollowreach's greatest cavern: vaulted halls of living rock, still lakes, glowing growths. | **Act 3:** the most beautiful and the most alien place in the game. |
| **The Ashen Delvings** | The burned-out mines of the Mine-Kings. Their tunnels run beneath the mountains towards home. | **Act 4:** the way out, and the final challenge. |
| **Hearthmere** *(new)* | Friendly territory beyond the mountains. | The goal. Also where the family-rescue story could begin. |

## People and powers

| Name | Who they were |
|---|---|
| **The Mine-Kings of Varrow** | An ancient dynasty that dug the Delvings and traded with the empire that built Gloamhold. Their carved seals appear on doors deeper down. |
| **The Hearthless King** | The last Mine-King, who let the great forge-hearth of Varrow go cold. Why the Delvings are ash is the deep mystery of the game. A possible final boss or ghostly presence. |
| **The empire** *(new: "the Graven Throne")* | The fallen empire that built Gloamhold. Its symbols, sentencing carvings and machinery fill Act 1. |
| **The Gaolwardens** *(new)* | The order that still runs Gloamhold's prison, now half-monstrous. The Gaoler is one of them. |

## Floor names for Act 1 *(new suggestions)*
- **Floor 1, The Penance Cells** (the Prison)
- **Floor 2, The Weeping Cistern** (the Cistern)
- **Floor 3, The Cinder Foundry** (the Foundry). Boss: **the Furnace Warden**

## Words for systems *(new suggestions)*
- **Tallow** (run currency): candle stubs. You spend them on light and on bargains, and lose them when you die. It ties into the light-and-shadow mechanic.
- **Embers** (legacy currency): the last warmth of the old hearths, kept between runs. It ties into the Hearthless King.
- **The Drop**: the pits, and being sent into them. "Dropped" guards might be found again further down.
- **Refuges**: safe rooms between floors (see the mechanics review).
